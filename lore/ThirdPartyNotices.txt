Fluid Speech Mini (Core ML)
https://huggingface.co/FluidVoice-Labs/fluid-speech-mini-coreml

Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Fluid Speech Mini by FluidVoice (FluidVoice Labs).

Built on NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3
(https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3, CC BY 4.0) and
Moondream Parakeet Ultra (https://huggingface.co/moondream/parakeet-ultra,
CC BY 4.0). The decoder and joint network are Parakeet Ultra's. The audio
preprocessor is from FluidInference's Parakeet v3 Core ML package, also
CC BY 4.0.

Lore downloads the model unmodified and runs it on this iPhone to transcribe
English recordings.
